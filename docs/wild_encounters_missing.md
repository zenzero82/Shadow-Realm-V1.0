- Omanyte/Omastar family
- Kabuto/Kabutops family
- Aerodactyl family
- Maractus family
- Sigilyph family
- Tirtouga/Carracosta family
- Archen/Archeops family
- Stufful/Bewear family
- Oranguru family
- Passimian family
- Honedge line
- Galarian Slowpoke line
- Impidimp line
- Spiritomb
- Lapras
- Girafarig line
- Shuckle
- Heracross (headbutt-only)
- All fossil Pokemon
- Burmy line (headbutt-only)
- Cherrim line (headbutt-only)
- Chatot
- Carnivine
- Pyroar line
- Slaking line
- Kecleon
- Tropius
- Squawkabilly
- Grafaiai line
- Brambleghast line
- Bombirdier
- Flamigo
- Silvally line
- Sudowoodo line
- Gholdengo line

Headbutt-only note: Heracross, Burmy line, and Cherrim line are allowed only via headbutt encounters (see `src/data/headbutt_encounters.h`).

Other exclusions for encounter placement (per request): starters, legendaries, mythicals, shadow species, and special forms such as Mega/Gmax/Totem.
